# Slug asset brief

## Role and footprint

- Environment-spawned garden animal; never a placeable or purchasable block.
- Runtime footprint is roughly 0.4 × 0.18 garden cells after scaling.
- Origin is at ground-level center and the model faces runtime `+Z`.
- The separate root, foot, rear, middle, mantle, head, and feeler pivots support
  the runtime glide/body-wave animation without a skeletal rig.

## Public references

- [University of Minnesota Extension](https://extension.umn.edu/yard-and-garden-insects/slugs): soft legless body, two feeler pairs, eye-bearing upper feelers, slime-assisted movement, and cool/moist/shaded activity.
- [Royal Horticultural Society](https://www.rhs.org.uk/advice/profile?PID=228&cID=597): damp-evening activity and an ecological role that can include decomposing organic matter.
- [Natural History Museum Slime Search](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/take-part/monitor-and-encourage-nature/project-archive/slime-search.html): readable mantle, foot/sole, body taper, and keel variations across real slug forms.

The references inform anatomy and ecology only. The asset is an original
Gredice design: an amber-brown segmented silhouette, moss-green saddle mantle,
gold mantle spots, cream foot, oversized readable feelers, and broad low-poly
forms tuned for the cozy orthographic/isometric camera. No geometry, texture,
silhouette, or animation was traced, imported, or copied from another game.
