# Google Play closed-test handoff

`google-play/hr-HR` contains the proposed listing and review notes for the Android Auto POI feasibility build. It is intentionally not release-complete: the screenshot directory contains a capture checklist rather than fabricated images.

A signed Delivery release must stay blocked until genuine phone and Desktop Head Unit screenshots are captured from the exact candidate build, the `POI` category is accepted in a Play closed test, a separate upload key is configured, and the Delivery Play app-signing certificate is published through Digital Asset Links.

See [`docs/android-play-release.md`](../../../docs/android-play-release.md) for the signed handoff, Digital Asset Links, and closed-track procedure.
