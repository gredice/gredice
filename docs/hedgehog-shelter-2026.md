# Hedgehog and garden shelter (#4977)

`HedgehogShelter` is a low arched timber shelter with an open entrance and bedding. The Croatian catalogue entry is **Sklonište za ježa**, 70 sunflowers, one tile, height 0.49 and a 0.9 square hitbox. It remains placeable throughout the year, supports no objects and cannot be placed on water. `Hedgehog` is a visitor, not a separately purchasable block. It has no feeding, collectible, crop-health or yield effects.

First-party references inspected: Rabbit for the small faceted animal and restrained face, RabbitHutch for a readable entrance, FallenLog for warm timber and a grounded silhouette. Both sources are original Blender assets generated by `assets/scripts/generate-hedgehog.py`; `-- --check` audits the saved sources. The animal uses named rigid pivots and three authored NLA clips: `HedgehogIdle` (breathing), `HedgehogWalk` (alternating feet) and `HedgehogSniff` (head movement).

## Habitat and lifecycle

The authenticated and public garden scenes mount one population owner alongside existing wildlife. At most one hedgehog visits a scene, even with many shelters. The shared seasonal state enables autumn visits; known rain/snow suppress visits. A winter or raised/water-facing shelter remains decorative. The public viewer respects its weather-visualization override.

Habitat planning reuses semantic animal surfaces, occupied-cell detection and the existing squirrel/ground pathfinder. It considers at most eight deterministically ranked shelters and twelve nearby route candidates per shelter, retaining up to three safe routes. Each path search uses ground within four tiles of the shelter. Paths are checked every 0.08 units with 0.26 clearance around the full body, reject water, occupied cells and abrupt height changes, and cannot take a detour longer than four units. A habitat requires two safe routes and a level free entrance. Invalid habitats do not run a retry loop.

The authored entrance is a special corridor belonging only to this shelter, from local z=-0.15 to z=-0.8. Geometry-ray tests verify its body clearance; ordinary routes keep the shelter cell blocked. The visitor idles inside, emerges, walks to one safe target, sniffs/pauses, follows the same verified route back, and retires inside. Planned active visits remain below 40 seconds, followed by a four-minute cooldown using the shared scene deadline scheduler. Habitat revisions remount against the new safe route, so blocking the entrance removes the old visitor immediately.

The shared scene clock supports repeatable frozen poses. Visibility suspension pauses active visit time without a resume jump. Slow visible frames retain elapsed time so visits do not extend on busy devices. Low/constrained quality requests and samples poses at most 15 times per second; other tiers use at most 24. This sampling cap also applies when unrelated scene effects request extra frames. Reduced motion holds a static visitor at the entrance, while the visit and cooldown still finish. No continuous render lease is retained during cooldown.

## Verification and release

Eleven focused tests cover source/model/preview hashes, catalogue/placement rules, all exported clips, corridor geometry, four shelter rotations, water/occupied/raised entrances, deterministic routes and the single-visitor cap. The browser suite covers 29 cases: day/night rotations, weather, small canvas, raised supports, picking/crop controls, picker publication/price/drag, low/high frozen remounts, cached-resource cleanup, entrance mutation, winter gating, offscreen pause/resume, full return/cooldown/reappearance, reduced motion and three close-up poses. Both quality captures measured one actor with six meshes, 444 triangles and zero shadow casters; this is geometry cost, not a GPU timing benchmark.

Eight preview renders generated four cover and top-down orientations; all ten published-image candidates have transparent backgrounds. Game/Garden/WWW typechecks, scoped importer types and offline plan, lint, the 1,985-test full game suite and the Garden production build passed.
