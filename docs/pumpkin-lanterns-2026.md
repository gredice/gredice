# Carved pumpkin lanterns (#4969)

Two original faces extend the approved ribbed HarvestPumpkin family: `PumpkinLanternSmile` (osmijeh) and `PumpkinLanternWink` (namigivanje). Each is a one-tile decoration, 0.55 high, with a 0.9 square hitbox, priced at 55 sunflowers. They do not support other blocks or placement on water. Owned lanterns remain available throughout the year; there is no event-expiry or nighttime purchase restriction.

First-party references inspected: HarvestPumpkinSquatOrange for ribs and palette, WoodenHandLantern for warm nighttime behavior, and GardenBrazier for the bounded emissive/light pattern. Sources are separate editable Blender files under `assets/game-assets`, regenerated by `assets/scripts/generate-pumpkin-lanterns.py`; `-- --check` audits the saved sources. Real boolean apertures open into an opaque interior. Their upward-sloping cuts and diagonal front keep the faces visible from the garden camera. Catalogue covers use the authored front.

Both have bounds 0.695 × 0.695 × 0.546, no textures, transparent surfaces, embedded lights or animation. Base rendering uses three mesh draws per lantern; weather overlays use the existing solid-surface helpers. Source materials remain cached and immutable. Each runtime owns its emissive material, which the shared GardenNightLight registry drives from the normal scene clock. There is no per-item flicker or added bloom. Point lights never cast shadows and share the existing global quality budget: 4 on low/auto, 8 on medium/custom, 20 on high. A browser scene with 38 pumpkin lanterns plus one existing lantern registered 39 lights and activated exactly 4 on low / 20 on high, all without shadow casting. These are light counts, not a GPU timing benchmark.

The 4×4 review garden includes real crop geometry, a bench and an existing lantern. Validation passed: four focused GLB/catalogue/placement tests (including rays through the carved apertures), 25 browser cases covering rotations, day/night, weather, small low-quality canvas, raised supports, picking and crop interaction, light caps, January use after the event, and picker publication/price/drag behavior. Sixteen preview renders produced the four cover and top-down orientations for each asset. All 13 review captures were inspected, and all 20 preview files have transparent backgrounds. Game/Garden/WWW typechecks, scoped importer types and offline plan, scoped/package lint, and snapshot-camera tests passed.

Hashes, draft metadata, model costs and preview paths are recorded in `docs/pumpkin-lanterns-2026/release-manifest.json`. Capture scenes use a frozen September 23 clock; public previews use October 22. Publication stays gated by #5000: deploy the reviewed Garden and WWW revisions, verify the model/images and prices, create/read back the draft catalogue entries, then publish and verify authenticated purchase/place/rotate/reload. No live catalogue writes or deployment were performed.
